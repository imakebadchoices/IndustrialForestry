# Moistener

The **Moistener** is an odd little machine: it does its work slowly, in the dark, and needs **no engine power at all** — only water and time. Its job is to grow things that thrive on damp and decay.

## What it makes

- **Mycelium**, from wheat or seeds and water — the block mushroom farms are grown on.
- **Mossy Cobblestone** and **Mossy Stone Bricks**, by letting their plain counterparts moulder.

## How it works

The Moistener keeps a **reservoir** of resources and pulls the most pristine item from it into a **working slot** each cycle. As it works, wheat degrades through stages — **Wheat → Mouldy Wheat → Decaying Wheat → Mulch** — and each further stage of decay makes for *better* mycelium production. Once an item reaches Mulch it is useless to the Moistener and should be pumped out to make room.

Two things matter for a good setup:

- **Keep it dark.** The Moistener works best in low light — many players build it into a sealed, unlit room.
- **Keep it watered.** A water tank feeds the process; pipe water in or refill it by hand.

Because it needs no engine, the Moistener is one of the cheapest machines to run continuously — you simply feed it wheat and water and collect the results.
