# Charcoal & Wood Piles

Forestry offers a bulk way to turn wood into fuel: the **Charcoal Pit**, a seamless multiblock that chars a pile of logs into **Charcoal** all at once. It pairs with [tree breeding](arboriculture.md), which supplies the logs.

## The Charcoal Pit

The pit is built by enclosing a core of logs completely inside **wall blocks**, then igniting it. With no air gaps, the wood chars rather than burns, and the enclosed pile converts to Charcoal.

The **wall material matters**: different blocks yield different amounts of charcoal per log, so sturdier, denser materials are worth using for a bigger return. The reaction also leaves **Ash** behind as a byproduct, itself a useful crafting ingredient.

The whole structure converts in one operation, so a large pit can char many logs at once.

## Fireproof wood

Forestry also lets you make **fireproof** versions of its woods — logs and planks that won't burn, useful near lava and in the Nether. See [Tree Breeding](arboriculture.md) for the wood types themselves.
