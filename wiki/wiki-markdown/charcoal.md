# Charcoal & Wood Piles

Forestry offers a bulk way to turn wood into fuel: the **Charcoal Pit**, a seamless multiblock that chars a pile of logs into **Charcoal** all at once. It is a natural companion to [tree breeding](arboriculture.md), giving all those farmed logs somewhere useful to go.

## The Charcoal Pit

The pit is built by enclosing a core of logs completely inside **wall blocks**, then igniting it. With no air gaps, the wood chars rather than burns, and the enclosed pile converts to Charcoal.

The **wall material matters**: different blocks yield different amounts of charcoal per log, so sturdier, denser materials are worth using for a bigger return. The reaction also leaves **Ash** behind as a byproduct, itself a useful crafting ingredient.

Because the whole structure converts in one operation, a Charcoal Pit scales far better than feeding logs through furnaces one stack at a time — build it large and process an entire tree farm's output in a single burn.

## Fireproof wood

Related to the charcoal path, Forestry lets you make **fireproof** versions of its woods — logs and planks that won't burn. Beyond the obvious safety near lava and in the Nether, fireproof wood is handy precisely *because* the charcoal and engine systems deal in fire so often. See [Tree Breeding](arboriculture.md) for the wood types themselves.
