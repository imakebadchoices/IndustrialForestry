# Thermionic Fabricator

The **Thermionic Fabricator** is the machine that turns glass into Forestry's electronic components. It melts glass down into a molten pool and casts it — together with metal ingots — into the **electron tubes** and **circuit boards** that upgrade machines and drive much of the mod's automation, as well as decorative stained glass.

## Heat and power

Unlike the other factory machines, the Fabricator works on **heat**. It runs on **Forge Energy (FE)**, and the power you feed it is converted into temperature: supply more and it heats up faster, supply too little and the heat slowly bleeds away. A recipe only melts and casts once the Fabricator is hot enough for the material in it.

## How it works

- **Meltable slot** — glass, glass panes, sand, or sandstone go here to be melted into the internal molten-glass buffer.
- **Recipe grid** — arrange the component you want to make.
- **Cast slot** — holds the cast a recipe needs (for example, a Wax Cast when making stained glass).
- **Output** — the finished part; picking it up consumes the molten glass and ingredients it needs.

## Why it matters

Electron tubes are the raw material of **circuit boards**, and circuit boards are how you upgrade machines like the [Centrifuge](centrifuge.md) and [Squeezer](squeezer.md) and configure the [Multifarm](farming.md). Setting up a Fabricator early is what unlocks the rest of Forestry's automation.
