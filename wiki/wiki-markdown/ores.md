# Ores & Resources

Forestry adds two ores of its own to world generation — **Apatite** and **Tin** — which supply the fertilizer and metals the rest of the mod runs on. (Copper, which classic Forestry once added, is now a vanilla ore, so Forestry no longer provides its own.)

## Apatite

**Apatite** is a phosphate mineral and the backbone of Forestry's fertilizer. It generates in large but uncommon veins, mines with a pickaxe, and drops **1–4 apatite gems** per ore — Fortune increases the yield, and Silk Touch collects the ore block itself. Apatite feeds directly into **Fertilizer**, which the [Multifarm](farming.md) and [Fermenter](fermenter.md) consume, and it is also an ingredient in [mail stamps](mail.md).

Both a stone and a **deepslate** variant generate, and mining the ore rewards a little experience.

## Tin

**Tin** generates much like copper does and smelts into **Tin Ingots**. Tin is combined with copper to make **Bronze**, the workhorse metal for Forestry's early machines and tools, and tin also goes into components like the Tin Electron Tube. It too has a **deepslate** variant.

## Uses

- **Apatite → Fertilizer** → farms and fermentation.
- **Tin (+ Copper) → Bronze** → machine casings, gears, and tools.
