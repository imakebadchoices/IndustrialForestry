# Access & Ownership

Most Forestry machines and multiblocks remember **who placed them**. That ownership is what protects your apiaries, farms, and machines on a shared server, and it is shown on a dedicated **Owner** tab in the block's interface.

## Ownership

When you place an owned block, Forestry records you as its owner. Open the block and you'll find an **Owner ledger** — one of the little tabs down the side of the GUI — displaying who owns it. Ownership travels with the block's data, so it persists as the block is saved and loaded.

Ownership underpins the mod's access behaviour: it is how the game knows whose bees are whose, and which player a machine's protections and interactions belong to. On a multiplayer server this keeps one player's breeding operation from being disturbed by another's.

> In classic Forestry this tab offered several named access levels (shared, restricted, and private). Community Edition has simplified that system down to straightforward ownership — the block knows its owner, shown on the Owner tab.
