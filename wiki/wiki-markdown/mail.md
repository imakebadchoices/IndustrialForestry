# Mail — Postal System

The mail module is an in-game postal network. Players send **letters** and **parcels** to one another — or to automated trade stations — across a server, with a small set of items and blocks that make it work. (Exactly how delivery happens remains a mystery; the leading theory involves specially trained postal bees.)

## Mailbox

The **Mailbox** is your address on the network. Place one down — somewhere accessible near home is customary — and letters sent to you are delivered into it, to be collected at your leisure. Any items posted to you sit safely inside until you open the package. A mailbox can be set to **private**, which stops other players both from peeking inside and from breaking it.

## Letters

A **Letter** is what you actually send. You write it, address it to a player or trade station, and can tuck **items** into it as well as a note — so a "letter" doubles as a parcel. Stamp it to pay for delivery, drop it in a mailbox, and it makes its way to the recipient.

## Stamps

**Stamps** are postage, and they come in denominations of increasing value (1n, 2n, and up). A letter needs enough stamp value attached to cover its delivery. Stamps are crafted from **apatite**, paper, and honey drops, which ties the postal system neatly into the rest of Forestry's resources.

## Trade Station

The **Trade Station** turns the postal system into an automated shop. Set one up and it will fulfil orders that arrive by mail — exchanging items for stamps or other goods without you being present — which lets players run a trade network across a server. Available trade stations can be browsed in the **Catalogue** or listed in-game with `/mail trades`.
