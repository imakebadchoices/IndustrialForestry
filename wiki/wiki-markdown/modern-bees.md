# Modern Bees

Modern Bees integrates Forestry with [Modern Industrialization](https://modrinth.com/mod/modern-industrialization) and [Create Addition](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-crafts-additions).  Both are optional dependencies: the tin drill and the MI fuel mapping only take effect when MI is installed, the liquid-blaze-burner fuels only take effect when Create Addition is installed, and the furnace fuel buckets work in any furnace without any other install.

## Tin drill

A tin drill is added, crafted from MI components. MI's Quarry consumes it and produces sand, clay, dirt variants, mud, moss, Apatite Ore, and Bog Earth. Its recipes only load when Modern Industrialization is present.

## Fluids as MI fuel

Forestry's fluids can be burned as fuel in Modern Industrialization machinery; bio ethanol, biomass, seed oil, short mead, honey, and juice, each with its own energy value.

## Furnace fuels

Bio ethanol, biomass, and seed oil buckets burn as furnace fuel.

## Fluids in Create Addition's liquid blaze burner

The bio ethanol, biomass, and seed oil fluids themselves burn in [Create Addition](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-crafts-additions)'s liquid blaze burner, piped straight in as fluid rather than fed as buckets. The burn times match the furnace-fuel values.
