# Extra Bees

> **Requires this fork of Forestry.** Extra Bees does not yet run against upstream Forestry: Community Edition. It relies on data-driven taxonomy, bee effects, and recipe-output changes that are still awaiting review upstream. It works today on this fork, and will run against upstream once those changes are merged.

Extra Bees is an experimental reimplementation of Binnie's [Extra Bees](https://github.com/ForestryMC/Binnie), rebuilt on Forestry: Community Edition's data-driven genetics interfaces. It is bundled into the mod jar and needs no separate download.

It adds 122 bee species and 174 mutations covering metallic, gemstone, mineral, and elemental lines. They breed with the same tools and rules as the base bees. In JEI the species are grouped under `@extrabees`.

The port is at an early alpha stage, and its species, mutations, and products may still change.
