# Extra Bees

Extra Bees is an experimental reimplementation of Binnie's [Extra Bees](https://github.com/ForestryMC/Binnie), rebuilt on Forestry: Community Edition's data-driven genetics interfaces. It runs on Forestry: Community Edition as its own add-on jar; the species data is bundled in, so there is no separate datapack to install.

It adds 122 bee species and 174 mutations covering metallic, gemstone, mineral, and elemental lines. They breed with the same tools and rules as the base bees. In JEI the species are grouped under `@extrabees`.

The port is at an early alpha stage, and its species, mutations, and products may still change.
