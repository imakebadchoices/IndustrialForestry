# Extra Bees

Extra Bees is an experimental reimplementation of Binnie's [Extra Bees](https://github.com/ForestryMC/Binnie), rebuilt on Forestry: Community Edition's data-driven genetics interfaces. It runs on Forestry: Community Edition as its own add-on jar; the species data is bundled in, so there is no separate datapack to install.

It adds 113 bee species and 147 mutations covering metallic, gemstone, mineral, and elemental lines. They breed with the same tools and rules as the base bees. In JEI the species are grouped under `@extrabees`.

Where base Forestry has since grown a branch of its own that covers the same ground, Extra Bees defers to it rather than shipping a parallel one: the ancient line breeds out of base's Relic bees, the water and nether lines start from base's Aquatic and Embittered hive bees, and the leaf line starts from base's Lush bees.

The port is at an early alpha stage, and its species, mutations, and products may still change.
