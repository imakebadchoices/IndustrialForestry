# Core Materials

Underneath the bees, trees, and machines sits a layer of **shared materials** that nearly every part of Forestry is built from. You will craft these constantly, so it is worth knowing what they are and where they come from.

## Casings

**Casings** are the chassis of Forestry's machines. Most machine recipes start with one:

- **Sturdy Casing** — the standard machine base, built around bronze.
- **Hardened Casing** — a tougher casing for higher-tier blocks.
- **Impregnated Casing** — made with seed oil, used for wooden bee equipment like the Apiary.
- **Flexible Casing** — a softer casing used where a machine needs to bend the rules.

## Metals and gears

Forestry's own metal chain runs on **Tin** and **Bronze**. Tin is smelted from [tin ore](ores.md); alloyed with copper it becomes **Bronze**, which makes ingots, **Gears** (bronze, copper, and tin), and a full set of bronze **tools**. Gears in particular turn up in a great many recipes.

## Electronics

Automation runs on electronics made in the [Thermionic Fabricator](fabricator.md):

- **Electron Tubes** — cast from molten glass and a metal, in several varieties.
- **Circuit Boards** — assembled from electron tubes with a **Soldering Iron**, these are the programmable upgrades that tune machines and configure [farms](farm-structure.md).

## Fertilizer and soils

- **Fertilizer** (Apatite Fertilizer) and **Compost** — feed the [Multifarm](farming.md) and the [Fermenter](fermenter.md), and speed plant growth.
- **Bog Earth** and **Humus** — specialty soils made in the [Carpenter](carpenter.md), used by peat bogs and farms.
- **Peat** and **Bituminous Peat** — solid fuels for the [Peat Engine](energy.md), harvested from bogs, leaving **Ash**.

## Waxes and containers

**Beeswax** and **Refractory Wax** (from the [Centrifuge](centrifuge.md)) make capsules and the Wax Casts the Fabricator needs, while cartons and capsules carry Forestry's fluids around.

## Handy tools

- **Wrench** — rotates and dismantles Forestry machines.
- **Pipette** — draws liquid out of a machine's tank.
- **Portable Analyzer** — reads the genome of a bee, sapling, or butterfly in the field (see [Beekeeping Tools](bee-tools.md)).
- **Forester's Manual** — an in-game guide to the mod.
