# Rainmaker

The **Rainmaker** is a weather machine. Placed in the world and fed the right charge, it changes the weather to order — useful when your bees, trees, or farms would rather it were raining (or not).

## How it works

The Rainmaker runs on **charges**, inserted by right-clicking with one in hand or by piping them in:

- an **Iodine Charge** makes it **start raining**;
- a **Dissipation Charge** **stops the rain**.

Both charges are crafted in the **[Carpenter](carpenter.md)**. The Rainmaker itself needs **no power** to operate — just the charges.

## Rain Tank

A quieter companion to the Rainmaker is the **Rain Tank**, which simply collects **water** into an internal buffer whenever it is placed outside in the rain. It needs no energy to fill or to bottle its water into containers, making it a cheap, passive water source for machines like the [Squeezer](squeezer.md), [Fermenter](fermenter.md), and [Multifarm](farming.md) that thirst for it.
