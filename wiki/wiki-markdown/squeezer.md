# Squeezer

The **Squeezer** presses liquids out of items. It is the machine that turns raw bee and tree products into the fluids the rest of Forestry runs on.

## What it makes

- **Honey** — from honey drops, the backbone of the [biofuel chain](energy.md).
- **Seed Oil** — from seeds, needed by many [Carpenter](carpenter.md) recipes.
- **Juice** — from fruit, feedstock for the [Fermenter](fermenter.md).
- It can also reclaim the liquid from filled capsules and cans, occasionally recovering a little of the container material (such as an ingot) as a byproduct.

## How it works

Items to be pressed go into the **resource area**; the fluid collects in the **liquid tank**, from which you can pipe it out or draw it into containers via the container slots. Some recipes also drop a solid **byproduct** (Mulch is common) into a separate slot.

## Upgrades

The Squeezer has sockets for **circuit boards** (built from electron tubes in the [Thermionic Fabricator](fabricator.md)). Fitting the right board tunes the machine — trading power draw for speed or efficiency — so a well-upgraded Squeezer keeps pace with a large apiary or orchard. Like the other factory machines it runs on **Forge Energy (FE)**.
