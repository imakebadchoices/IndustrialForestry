# Backpacks & Crates

Forestry adds two portable storage systems that quietly solve the two biggest inventory problems of a busy base: **backpacks**, which collect items by category as you play, and **crates**, which compress a whole stack of one item into a single slot.

## Backpacks

A backpack is a class-restricted container: each kind only accepts a certain category of items, and non-matching items simply won't go in. Their real trick is that **items you pick up are sorted into a matching backpack automatically**, keeping your main inventory clear. (Matching stacks already in your inventory are topped off first, so you always keep some on hand.)

### Categories

- **Apiarist's** — bees, combs, and apiculture items.
- **Arborist's** — saplings, wood, and arboriculture items.
- **Lepidopterist's** — butterflies and related items.
- **Forester's** — logs and foresting materials.
- **Miner's** — ores, ingots, and gems.
- **Digger's** — dirt, sand, and soft blocks.
- **Hunter's** — mob drops.
- **Adventuring** — miscellaneous adventuring loot.
- **Builder's** — building blocks.

### Modes

Crouch and right-click (away from a chest) to cycle a backpack through four modes:

- **Normal** — receives items after your matching inventory stacks are full.
- **Locked** — receives nothing.
- **Receiving** — like Normal, but crouch-right-clicking a chest *fills* the backpack from that chest instead of emptying into it.
- **Resupply** — like Normal, but also automatically restocks your inventory from the backpack — the classic way to carry a thousand arrows or a huge buffer of cobble in a single slot.

In the normal case, crouch-right-click a chest to **dump the backpack's contents** into it. The naturalist backpacks (Apiarist's, Arborist's, Lepidopterist's) tie directly into the breeding systems.

### Upgrade tiers

Each backpack has larger **Woven** versions, and beyond them **Ender-Woven** and **Chorus-Woven** tiers, each raising capacity — so a backpack grows with you rather than being replaced.

## Crates

A **Crate** packs a large quantity of a single item type into one crated item, ideal for bulk storage and transport. Forestry registers crated variants of many of its products (bee combs among them). Crates are filled and emptied at a **[Carpenter](carpenter.md)**, making them a natural companion to a well-automated base.
