# Still

The **Still** is the second step of the biofuel chain. It **distills Biomass into Bio Ethanol** — a more energy-dense fuel — refining the raw output of the [Fermenter](fermenter.md) into something that burns longer and hotter.

## Usage

Biomass goes into the Still's input tank; as it processes, **Bio Ethanol** builds up in the output tank. You can draw the ethanol off with pipes or fill containers from it directly. Refining is lossy — you get less ethanol out than biomass in — but the ethanol's higher energy value more than makes up for it when burned in a [Biogas Engine](energy.md).

The Still runs on **Forge Energy (FE)**, so it needs power of its own to do the refining.

## Fuel chain

The full chain is short and self-contained:

**[Fermenter](fermenter.md)** (plant matter → Biomass) → **Still** (Biomass → Bio Ethanol) → **[Biogas Engine](energy.md)** (Bio Ethanol → Forge Energy).

You can burn Biomass in the Biogas Engine directly and skip the Still, but distilling to ethanol first gets far more power out of the same plant matter — worth the extra machine once your farms are producing surplus.
