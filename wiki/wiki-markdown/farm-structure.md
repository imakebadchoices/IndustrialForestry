# Farm Structure

A [Multifarm](farming.md) is a multiblock: you assemble it from farm blocks into a hollow box with a band of soil across the middle, and the structure forms itself once the shape is valid. This page covers the blocks and how they go together.

## The Farm Block

The **Farm Block** is the structural body of the farm — you need a good number of them, and **the entire third layer must be farm blocks**. Every other position in the walls can be a farm block *or* one of the component blocks below, so you slot the components in wherever is convenient.

Farm Blocks come in many decorative materials — stone brick and its mossy, cracked and chiselled variants, plain brick, sandstone, quartz, nether brick and more — and they can be freely mixed, so a farm can be made to look like part of your build rather than a bare box.

## Component blocks

Four special blocks give the farm what it needs to run. Each replaces a Farm Block somewhere in the structure:

- **Farm Valve** — the water intake. Pipe or pour water in here to hydrate the soil; a large farm may want more than one.
- **Farm Gearbox** — the power connection, where the farm draws the **Forge Energy (FE)** it runs on.
- **Farm Hatch** — the item connection, for piping harvested produce out (and fertilizer in).
- **Farm Control** — the farm's brain and interface: open it to install farm logic, set what grows on each face, and load fertilizer.

1. Decide on a size — farms are built to set footprints (commonly 3×3 through 5×5 across).
2. Lay the walls from **Farm Blocks**, keeping the whole third layer solid farm blocks.
3. Swap in at least one **Valve**, **Gearbox**, **Hatch**, and **Control** where they fit.
4. Fill the central band with the appropriate soil (dirt, sand, bog earth, and so on for the crop you intend).
5. Supply water and power, install the farm logic in the Control, and add fertilizer — the farm starts working on its own.

See **[Multifarm](farming.md)** for the farm types and what each one grows.
