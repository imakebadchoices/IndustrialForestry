# Engines

Forestry's machines run on **Forge Energy (FE)**, and its engines are how you make that power from the mod's own resources rather than leaning on another mod. There are three, each suited to a different stage of the game.

Like all Forestry engines, they **heat up as they run** and work best kept in continuous operation — starting and stopping constantly wastes their warm-up. Point an engine at the machine or energy network you want to power and keep its fuel topped up.

## Biogas Engine

The Biogas Engine is the mainline generator for a developed base. It burns **liquid fuels**, and it accepts a whole range of them — so almost every fluid Forestry produces has somewhere to go:

- **Biomass** and **Bio Ethanol** — the purpose-made fuels from the [Fermenter](fermenter.md) and [Still](still.md); ethanol is the most energy-dense.
- **Honey** and **Juice** — squeezed from bee and tree products, a convenient use for surplus.
- **Seed Oil** — a modest fuel.
- **Water** and **Milk** — work in a pinch, but burn weakly and quickly.

(The exact power and burn time of each fuel are tunable in Forestry's config.)

## Peat-fired Engine

The Peat Engine burns **solid** fuel instead of liquid: **Peat** and the richer **Bituminous Peat**, both harvested or produced from bogs (see the [peat farm](farming.md)). It is a dependable early- and mid-game generator that runs without any of the fluid infrastructure the Biogas Engine needs, and it leaves **Ash** behind as a byproduct.

## Clockwork Engine

The Clockwork Engine is the earliest option and needs no fuel at all — you **wind it by hand**, right-clicking to tighten its spring, and it delivers power in short bursts as it unwinds. Output rises with how tightly it is wound. It is ideal for small or intermittent loads before you have a fuel chain set up. Wind it too far, though, and it turns red and risks overwinding, so let it relax between windings.

## The fuel chain

The Biogas Engine's best fuels come from a short production line: the **[Fermenter](fermenter.md)** turns saplings and other plant matter into **Biomass**, and the **[Still](still.md)** refines that Biomass into **Bio Ethanol**. Feed either back into the Biogas Engine to close the loop.
